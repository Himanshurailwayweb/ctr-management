Proposed change will never directly overwrite the approved CTR.

1 Proposed Employee submits change
→
2 Review Officer verification
→

→
4 Digital Sign Final authorization
→
5 New CTR Version & PDF generated
Important

During the initial CTR data-entry stage, this workflow remains disabled. It will become active only after the station's existing CTR is verified and locked as the baseline version.
